# Canton Research Claim Ledger

## Decision

This ledger stores cross-topic conclusions separately from narrative notes. `EXPLICIT` means the cited section directly supports the claim, `INFERRED` means the reasoning combines cited facts, and `UNCLEAR` means the official corpus does not support one unqualified conclusion. Confidence rates documentation support, not implementation truth.
