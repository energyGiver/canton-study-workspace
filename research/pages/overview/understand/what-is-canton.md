## Three-line summary

1. Canton Network is a public Layer 1 blockchain that distributes transaction data selectively instead of replicating all state to every participant.
2. Synchronizers coordinate consensus while Participant Nodes store and validate only the ledger projection relevant to their hosted Parties.
3. Daml models multi-party authorization, visibility, and immutable contract lifecycles for privacy-sensitive workflows.
